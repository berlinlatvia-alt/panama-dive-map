Beginner & Amateur Scuba Guide

🤿 "Buzo" = Certified Scuba Diver

When an operator writes "$300 / buzo", it means "$300 per certified diver". They charge per person who breathes from scuba cylinders underwater. Non-diver companions who just want to sunbathe or snorkel usually pay much less ($50–$100).

Buceo vs Snorkel: Buceo is underwater scuba diving with compressed air tanks. Snorkeling is floating on the surface with a mask and breathing tube.
2 Inmersiones (2 Tanks): Standard day tour consists of 2 dives (~45 min each) separated by a 1-hour rest interval on the boat with fruits and refreshments.
Equipo Completo (Full Gear): Includes BCD buoyancy jacket, regulator, neoprene wetsuit, mask, and fins.
YES! Total beginners can dive in Panama. No prior license is required if you book a guided introductory program:
1. Discover Scuba Diving (DSD / "Try Scuba")

No certification required! You do a 20-minute briefing and dive hand-in-hand with an instructor down to 10–12 meters (35 feet).

2. Open Water Diver (OWD License)

The entry-level international scuba license (3-4 days). Qualifies you to dive to 18 meters (60 feet) worldwide with a buddy.

3. Snorkel Companion Passenger

Ride the dive boat, sunbathe on deck, and snorkel on shallow reefs while divers are below. Operators charge substantially less ($50–$100).

✓ #1 Best for Beginners: Portobelo (Caribbean)

Protected bay with zero oceanic swell. Shallow coral gardens from 3m–15m, crystal Caribbean waters, and the intact Beechcraft twin-engine airplane wreck sits in calm sand at 21m. Flat rate: $155/day all-inclusive.

⛔ Advanced Only: Coiba (Hannibal Bank) & Los Frailes

Open-ocean seamounts with roaring currents, 50m drop-offs, and schooling hammerheads. Requires 30+ logged dives and good buoyancy control. Not suitable for beginners.

❓ Can I dive if I have never tried scuba before and have zero license?

YES, 100%! Book "Discover Scuba Diving" (DSD). No license or study needed. A certified instructor trains you in 20 minutes, equips you, and holds your hand underwater in calm shallow reefs (under 12m).

❓ What if I'm not a strong swimmer?

You wear a BCD buoyancy jacket that inflates at the touch of a button to keep you floating effortlessly like a lifejacket at the surface. Gentle fin kicks move you underwater.

❓ How do I equalize ear pressure?

Just pinch your nose and blow gently every 1 meter down, just like landing on an airplane. It pops your ears and eliminates all pressure.

❓ Can non-divers come on the boat to snorkel?

Yes! Dive operators offer a passenger/snorkel rate ($50-$100). They sunbathe on deck, eat fresh fruit, and snorkel on top of the reef while divers are underwater.
